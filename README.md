# 🚀 CRM Lead Management & Automation Dashboard

> AI-Powered CRM Dashboard & Lead Automation using HubSpot, Google Sheets, Zapier, n8n, and Google Apps Script to streamline lead management, automate reporting, and improve business performance.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates a professional CRM Lead Management & Automation Dashboard designed to help businesses automate lead tracking, KPI reporting, workflow automation, and business reporting using Google Sheets, HubSpot, Zapier, Google Apps Script, and n8n.

The solution eliminates repetitive manual work, improves data accuracy, and delivers real-time business insights for faster and smarter decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Many businesses spend hours manually updating CRM records, tracking leads, preparing KPI reports, and generating business dashboards.

This manual process often leads to:
- Lost sales opportunities
- Slow follow-ups
- Reporting errors
- Duplicate records
- Poor visibility into business performance

This project automates the entire workflow to save time and improve productivity.


## ⭐ Why This Solution?

Unlike traditional spreadsheet-based reporting, this solution automatically:
- Collects and organizes CRM data
- Cleans duplicate records
- Tracks sales performance
- Updates KPI dashboards
- Sends automated Gmail notifications
- Generates executive reports

Result:
✅ Less manual work
✅ Better CRM accuracy
✅ Faster business decisions
